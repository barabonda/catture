# 🐈 CATTURE— 
### A Companion That Creates the First Action for Isolated Youth

---

## The Problem

The number of socially isolated and withdrawn youth in Korea has **more than doubled in the past two years**.  
Among them, **30% have remained isolated for over five years**.

This isn’t just taking a short break.  
They are **quietly disappearing from society**.

For them, the hardest thing is just one thing:  
**starting the first action**.

But people fear change.  
The moment they begin to think about it, they stop — and return to where they were.

---

## [The Solution

So **catture removes the time to hesitate.**

No text.  
Just **one photo** to start a conversation.

> “Take a picture of what you see right now.”

Press the camera button — that’s it.

Photos trigger action faster than text.  
Your body moves before your thoughts can stop you.

One photo. Then another.  
Before you know it, you’re stepping outside.

---
